# HR Policy: Employee Leave

**Department:** Human Resources
**Last Updated:** 2026-03-01
**Access Level:** All Employees

## 1. Annual Leave
Full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued monthly at a rate of 1.67 days/month. Unused leave can be carried over up to a maximum of 5 days into the next calendar year; anything beyond that is forfeited unless local law requires otherwise.

## 2. Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. A medical certificate is required for absences exceeding 3 consecutive days. Sick leave does not carry over to the next year.

## 3. Parental Leave
- Primary caregiver: 16 weeks paid leave
- Secondary caregiver: 6 weeks paid leave
- Leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption date.

## 4. Bereavement Leave
Up to 5 paid days for immediate family, 2 paid days for extended family.

## 5. Requesting Leave
All leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 5 business days in advance, except in emergencies. Manager approval is required before leave is confirmed.

## 6. Unpaid Leave
Employees may request unpaid leave of absence for up to 90 days, subject to manager and HR approval, for personal reasons not covered above.

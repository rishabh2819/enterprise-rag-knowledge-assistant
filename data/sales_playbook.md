# Sales Playbook — Enterprise Segment

**Department:** Sales
**Last Updated:** 2026-04-10
**Access Level:** Sales Team, Sales Management

## 1. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
- Company size: 500+ employees
- Industry focus: Financial Services, Healthcare, Technology
- Pain points: fragmented file storage, compliance risk, lack of audit trails

## 2. Discovery Call Framework
1. Confirm current tools in use (competitors: DriveShare, FileVault Corp)
2. Identify compliance requirements (SOC 2, HIPAA, GDPR)
3. Quantify pain: how many hours/week lost to file search or version conflicts?
4. Identify economic buyer and technical champion

## 3. Objection Handling
**"We already use DriveShare"** → Highlight superior audit logging and BYOK encryption, offer side-by-side migration assistance.

**"Too expensive"** → Reframe around cost of compliance violations and lost productivity; offer annual billing discount (15%).

**"Security concerns"** → Provide SOC 2 Type II report, offer security architecture review call with our Solutions Engineering team.

## 4. Deal Stages
1. Discovery
2. Technical Validation (POC — typically 2-4 weeks)
3. Proposal & Pricing
4. Security/Legal Review
5. Contract Negotiation
6. Closed Won

## 5. Discounting Guidelines
- Up to 10% discretionary discount for annual prepay
- Above 10% requires Sales Director approval
- Above 25% requires VP Sales approval
